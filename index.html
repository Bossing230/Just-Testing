Scan a pageSee how it works

From crooked photo to finished file

  1. Straighten

    Drag four corners onto the page. Flatbed flattens the angle and removes shadows.

  2. Read

    Headings, paragraphs, lists and tables are found and kept in order.

  3. Check

    The scan sits next to the result. Fix any word before you export.

  4. Download

    Word for text, Excel for tables. Both open ready to edit.

Word for handouts

Letters, notices, lesson sheets and forms become a .docx with real headings, paragraphs and bullet lists, not a picture of text.

Excel for lists

Attendance lists, schedules and score sheets become a .xlsx. Each row stays a row, each column stays a column.

Be aware: phone photos and handwriting are hard to read. Flatbed matches the structure and order of the page, not the exact position of every word, so always glance at the check step.
